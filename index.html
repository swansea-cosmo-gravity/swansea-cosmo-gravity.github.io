Cosmology & Gravity @ Swansea

Our research group explores fundamental questions in cosmology and gravity, from the early Universe to dark energy, dark matter, and gravitational waves. Our work connects theoretical ideas, ranging from quantum field theory in curved spacetime to string theory, with observational opportunities. We are actively involved in major international collaborations, including LISA, NANOGrav, SKAO, Einstein Telescope, and Euclid.

Research

The early Universe

Inflation and its multifield and string-theory realisations, quantum fields in de Sitter space, primordial black holes and the origin of matter.

Dark energy and dark matter

Quintessence and coupled dark sectors, dark matter production, and testing models against the newest cosmological data with Bayesian and machine-learning methods.

Gravitational waves

Primordial and stochastic backgrounds and how to detect them: pulsar timing arrays, astrometry, LISA, the Einstein Telescope, the SKA and new quantum-sensing detectors.

Strings, fields and gravity

String compactifications and phenomenology, supergravity, modified gravity and black holes, and what quantum gravity implies for cosmology.

Join us

We welcome PhD students, postdocs and visitors interested in cosmology, gravitational waves and string theory. When we have funded positions we advertise them on INSPIRE Jobs.

We are also glad to host researchers who bring their own funding, and to help strong candidates prepare fellowship applications with Swansea as host. Contact the faculty member whose work is closest to yours, ideally a few months before the deadline.
